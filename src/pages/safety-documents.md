# Fun Pro UK – Safety Documents

Every document below is the current build-time version supplied to venues,
event organisers and clients as part of the booking process:

- Risk Assessments – Covering potential hazards and the control measures in place
- Method Statements – Explaining how each activity and piece of equipment is delivered safely
- PAT Testing Certificates – Provided for relevant electrical equipment

Please ensure you refer to the most recent version of any document provided. If
you require additional documentation for your booking, contact the Fun Pro UK
team.
