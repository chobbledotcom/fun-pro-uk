  - title: How Sub Soccer Works
    body: >-
      1. Sit opposite your opponent at the LED Sub Soccer table.

      2. Kick the ball through the goals using only your feet, staying seated
      throughout.

      3. Score as many goals as you can before the timer runs out.

      4. Watch the automatic scoreboard track the action, then challenge
      colleagues and guests to beat your score.


      The game has a good story behind it, too. It was invented in Finland in
      2008 by Jarno Saarinen, who came up with the idea while trying to watch
      Euro 2008 and keep his young daughter entertained at the same time. What
      started as a ball kicked between two chairs has since spread to more than
      80 countries, and it's easy to see why once the first match kicks off.
