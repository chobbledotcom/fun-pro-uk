      ## Northampton Venues We Know Well


      Venue familiarity matters more than most people expect. Loading access,
      floor surfaces, power availability and timing windows all shape what is
      realistic on the day, and it is far easier to work these out in advance
      than on arrival.


      ### cinch Stadium at Franklin's Gardens


      Home of Northampton Saints, Franklin's Gardens is one of the county's main
      venues for conferences, award dinners and company celebrations, sitting
      five minutes from the M1 with substantial on-site parking. It has several
      distinct spaces, from suites and lounges through to executive boxes and an
      outdoor village area, and it accommodates several hundred attendees across
      them. Game choice varies a good deal depending on which space you are in.
      Boxes and lounges generally suit compact reaction games and photo booths,
      whilst the outdoor areas open up larger setups and fun day formats.


      ### Whittlebury Park


      Set in around 700 acres near Towcester, next to Silverstone Circuit,
      Whittlebury Park is used for residential conferences, training days and
      larger corporate away days. With over 100 acres of outdoor event space, it
      is one of the few venues in the county where you can genuinely run a
      multi-station outdoor fun day rather than squeezing activities into a
      function room. One practical point on estates this size: the distance
      between the load-in point and the actual event space matters more than the
      space itself, so it is worth confirming early.


      ### Delapré Abbey


      A historic house and grounds close to Northampton town centre, Delapré
      Abbey hosts meetings, conferences and company celebrations, with rooms
      taking up to around 100 people theatre-style and higher capacity for
      receptions. Heritage venues often carry restrictions on floor protection,
      access routes and fixings that modern event spaces do not, so raise your
      game choices with the venue team early and we can advise on what fits
      within those limits.

      ## Planning Game Hire for a Northampton Corporate Event

      Most game hire in Northampton bookings follow a similar pattern, but a few practical points are worth thinking through before you confirm your game choices

      Guest numbers and flow matter more than many people realise. Games need enough clear space to operate safely, plus room for a small queue to form without blocking walkways or fire exits. Sharing your floor plan with us early makes this much easier to solve.

      Your audience mix is just as important. A group spanning different ages, physical abilities and personalities needs several options rather than a single high-energy game that only appeals to part of the room. Think about who might feel left out if the entertainment skews too competitive or too physical, and consider pairing active games with lower-key alternatives.

      Your objectives should guide the choice too. Exhibition stands need games that pull footfall and start conversations quickly. ++[Team building](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/team-building-entertainment/)++  activities need to sustain engagement across several hours and encourage people to interact with colleagues they do not normally work alongside. Both are achievable, but they call for different approaches, and our guide to ++[team building games that actually work](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/news/team-building-events-games-and-activities-that-actually-work/)++ goes into more detail.

      Indoor or outdoor is a bigger decision here than in most cities, given how many estate and parkland venues the county has. Ground conditions, power access away from the main building, and a realistic weather contingency all need settling before the day.

      On logistics, most games need a standard mains socket and a clear floor area of roughly two to three metres. If your venue has unusual constraints, tell us and we can suggest alternatives that work within them. If branding is part of your brief, raise it as early as you can, because production needs lead time.
