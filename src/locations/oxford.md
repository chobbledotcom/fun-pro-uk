      ## Oxford Venues We Deliver To


      Oxford's event spaces range from large modern conference centres to
      Victorian halls and college buildings. The building you're using has a
      real bearing on which games will work. Loading access, lifts and stairs,
      floor surfaces, power points and timing windows all affect what's
      practical, so we're always happy to work with your venue team on the
      details.


      ### The King's Centre


      The King's Centre in Osney Mead is one of Oxford's largest conference
      venues. It has around 30,000 square feet of space, with two large halls
      and a set of smaller support rooms. The main halls are the Thames Hall and
      the Cotswold Hall, and the venue sits roughly ten minutes' walk from
      Oxford rail station. It hosts conferences, exhibitions and award
      celebrations. The large halls suit ++[branded exhibition
      games](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/branded-exhibition-games/)++
      and multi-game setups. On a busy exhibition floor, a game that draws a
      crowd helps your stand compete for attention.


      ### The Kassam Conference and Events Centre


      The Kassam Conference and Events Centre is one of the larger event spaces
      in Oxfordshire. Its Quadrangle Suite can be divided into three separate
      soundproofed rooms, and the concourse outside can host a small number of
      exhibition stands or work as a breakout and drinks reception area. The
      main suites are on the first floor, so it's worth sharing lift and access
      details with us early if you're considering larger games such as racing
      simulators or Grab A Grand. Compact reaction and prize games tend to work
      well on the concourse.


      ### Oxford Town Hall


      Oxford Town Hall is a Grade II* listed Victorian building in the city
      centre. Alongside the grand Main Hall, smaller rooms such as the Assembly
      Room and the Old Library are used for conferences, receptions and
      Christmas parties. Historic and listed buildings often come with tighter
      access, older floors and rules about what can be placed where. That means
      self-contained, freestanding games tend to be the easier fit. Photo
      booths, Roll & Bowl, reaction games and pub games are all reliable choices
      here.


      ### Other Venues Across Oxford and Oxfordshire


      We're also happy to deliver to college halls and university buildings,
      city centre hotels and business school event spaces. The same goes for the
      science and business parks around the county, including Oxford Science
      Park, Milton Park and Harwell Campus. If your venue isn't listed here,
      ++[get in touch](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/contact-fun-pro-uk/)++ and the
      team can advise on what's possible.

      ## Planning Game Hire for an Oxford Event

      There are a few practical considerations worth thinking through before you confirm your game choices.

      Guest numbers and flow matter more than many people realise. Games need enough clear space to operate safely, and you'll want room for a small queue to form without blocking walkways or fire exits. In older Oxford buildings, rooms and corridors are often narrower than in purpose-built venues. It's a good idea to share your floor plan with us early so we can help you work out what fits.

      Your audience mix is equally important. Oxford events often bring together people from very different backgrounds, whether that's researchers and commercial teams at a science park social or staff and their families at a summer fun day. A group that spans different ages, physical abilities and personalities needs a range of options rather than a single high-energy game that only appeals to some of your guests. Think about who might feel left out if the entertainment skews too competitive or too physical, and consider a mix of active games and lower-key options.

      Your event objectives should guide your choices too. Exhibition stands need games that draw footfall and start conversations quickly. A ++[team building](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/team-building-entertainment/)++ day or staff social needs activities that hold attention across several hours. They should also encourage people to mix with colleagues they don't usually spend time with. Both are achievable, but they call for different approaches, and it's worth discussing this with us before you decide. If you're organising a conference, our ++[conference game hire](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/conference-game-hire/)++ page covers the options in more detail.

      On logistics, most games need a standard mains power socket and a clear floor area of around two to three metres. When you get in touch, let us know about anything unusual, such as first-floor rooms, lift sizes, stairs, listed building rules or loading restrictions in the city centre. That helps us suggest indoor games that work within your venue's limits.

      If branding is part of your brief, raise it early. Branded games are one of the most effective ways to reinforce your company's presence at an exhibition or activation. Because we handle design and print in-house, you're dealing with one team from start to finish. Production needs lead time, and our ++[guide to branded game hire](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/news/the-event-planners-guide-to-branded-game-hire-what-it-is-how-it-works-and-whether-its-worth-it/)++ explains how the process works.
