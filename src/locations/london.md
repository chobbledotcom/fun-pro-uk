      ## Planning Game Hire for a London Corporate Event


      There are a few practical things worth thinking through before you confirm
      your game choices.


      Space and guest flow matter more than many people expect, and they matter
      more in London than almost anywhere else. Games need enough clear area to
      operate safely, and you'll want room for a small queue to form without
      blocking walkways or fire exits. Share your floor plan with us early and
      we can help you work out what fits comfortably rather than what fits
      technically.


      Access is the other consideration that catches London events out. Central
      venues often have narrow delivery windows, and office buildings bring
      goods lifts and restricted entrances into the picture. It's something we
      plan around from the start, so tell us the venue when you get in touch and
      we'll factor the logistics in before anything is confirmed.


      Your audience mix should guide the choices as much as the space does. A
      group spanning different ages, physical abilities and personalities needs
      a range of options rather than one high-energy game that appeals to a
      subset of your guests. Think about who might feel left out if the
      entertainment skews too competitive or too physical, and consider whether
      a mix of active games and lower-key options would serve the group better.


      Your objectives matter too. Exhibition stands need games that draw
      footfall and start conversations quickly. Team days need activities that
      sustain engagement across several hours and encourage people to interact
      with colleagues they might not normally spend time with. Both are
      achievable, but they call for different approaches, and it's worth talking
      through before you decide.


      On the practical side, most games run from a standard mains socket and
      need a clear floor area of around two to three metres. If branding is part
      of your brief, raise it early. Custom-wrapped games are one of the more
      effective ways to reinforce your presence at an exhibition or activation,
      and because we handle design and print in-house, you're dealing with one
      team throughout. Production lead time is needed, so the sooner you mention
      it, the better.
