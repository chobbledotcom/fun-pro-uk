## **About Edgbaston Stadium as an Events Venue**

Edgbaston sits around two miles from Birmingham city centre, with good road and rail access and on-site parking for over 500 cars. Free Wi-Fi runs throughout, and the stadium is open year-round for events rather than just on match days.

For event planners, the useful detail is the range. Edgbaston offers around 40 suites and rooms, hosting conferences from 10 delegates up to 800, and events of up to roughly 1,050 people. Most spaces look out over the pitch, several have private balconies, and on-site technical support is available. There's also the Edgbaston Plaza, an outdoor space of around 14,800 square metres, which is one of the largest outdoor event spaces in the city. Venue bookings are made directly through the Edgbaston events team.

## **Function Rooms at Edgbaston and the Games That Suit Them**

Different spaces work well for different kinds of entertainment. Once you know which suite your event is in, we can recommend games that fit the footprint, power setup, and style of the occasion.

### **The Banqueting Suite**

On Level 2, the Banqueting Suite is one of the largest spaces at Edgbaston, seating up to around 700 for a banquet and taking larger conferences and exhibitions too. It splits into three sections using built-in partitions, the East Suite, West Suite and Room With A View, each with natural daylight and its own bar, and the East and West can be combined. 

That flexibility suits a mix of entertainment zones: larger-footprint games like racing car simulators, ++[Batak Pro](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/birmingham/batak-pro-hire/)++ and ++[Batak Lite](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/birmingham/batak-lite-hire/)++ reaction games and ++[prize cranes](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/birmingham/prize-crane-hire/)++ work well in the open sections, while ++[photo booths](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/photo-booths-and-magic-mirrors/)++ sit nicely near a bar area for post-dinner entertainment. The private balcony overlooking the pitch is worth factoring into your guest flow.

### **The 1882 Suite**

Named after the year the club was founded, the 1882 Suite is a bright, versatile space popular for mid-sized events, with a flexible layout, private bar and pitch views. It suits a moderate spread of games rather than the largest kit. Shuffleboard and other ++[pub games](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/pub-games/)++, ++[retro arcade machines](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/arcade-games/)++, and ++[prize wheels](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/prize-wheel)++ all work comfortably here without crowding the room.

### **The Chair's Lounge and Meeting Rooms**

The Chair's Lounge is a self-contained space suited to board meetings and smaller workshops, and Edgbaston has a range of meeting rooms alongside it. Space is more limited in these rooms, so compact games work best. Smaller retro arcades, pub games and prize wheels fit neatly and work well as a break from the agenda rather than the main event.

### **Executive Boxes**

The executive boxes suit smaller gatherings and breakout sessions, offering privacy and pitch views. These are compact spaces, so we'd steer you towards games with a small footprint. A prize wheel or a smaller arcade machine gives guests something to gather around without dominating the room.

## **Planning Entertainment for an Edgbaston Event**

A few practical things are worth thinking about early. Once Edgbaston has confirmed your suite, it's helpful to share the floor plan with us. That way we can advise on which games fit comfortably, where power points sit relative to your layout, and how to keep walkways clear for guest flow. In a divisible space like the Banqueting Suite, it's also worth deciding early whether you want games in their own zone or spread across the room.

Timing matters too. As a working cricket ground, Edgbaston can have access windows tied to the fixture calendar, particularly through the summer season. Your events contact at the stadium will be able to tell you when loading is possible, and we'll work around whatever they confirm. Lift access to Level 2 is worth mentioning to us as well, since it affects how we plan the setup.

In terms of lead time, two to four weeks' notice is usually comfortable for most games. If your event falls in November or December, or lands near a major fixture, we'd recommend booking earlier, as those dates tend to fill up first.

It's also worth thinking about the mix of guests attending and choosing games that everyone can join in with. We'll happily talk through options that work for different ages, abilities, and comfort levels. You can read more about our approach on the corporate events page.
