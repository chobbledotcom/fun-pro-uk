      We deliver equipment to the venue you have booked. Knowing a venue in
      advance helps us plan access, timings and power, so it is always useful to

      ### Stadium MK and Arena MK


      Stadium MK is a 30,500 seat stadium and one of the larger conference and
      exhibition destinations in the region. The DoubleTree by Hilton Milton
      Keynes sits on site with a range of conference and event spaces, including
      a ballroom that takes up to 1,000 guests. Arena MK offers around 3,420
      square metres of indoor event space across three floors and is used
      regularly for exhibitions, product launches and national conferences.


      The practical difference matters when you are choosing games. Open arena
      space gives you room for larger setups, simulators and multiple branded
      games running side by side. Hotel function rooms suit compact, self
      contained games that do not need a run up. On site parking and the M1
      Junction 14 approach make load in reasonably painless, which is not
      something you can take for granted at city centre venues.


      ### Milton Keynes Conference Centre


      MKCC on Strudwick Drive in Oldbrook is a purpose built venue with a main
      auditorium seating up to 550, alongside smaller suites for breakout
      sessions.


      With auditorium led venues, games generally live in the reception and
      breakout areas rather than the main room. That works well if you plan for
      it. A reaction game or photo booth positioned where delegates pass during
      a break gives people something to do in the fifteen minutes between
      sessions, which is usually when energy dips and conversation stalls.


      ### Hotel and business park venues across MK


      Milton Keynes has a high concentration of conference hotels and business

      ## Planning Game Hire for a Milton Keynes Corporate Event

      Think about guest numbers and how people will move around the space. Games need a clear operating room plus somewhere for a queue to form without blocking a walkway or a fire exit. A floor plan sorts this out in a five minute conversation rather than on the day.

      Consider the mix of people attending. A group spanning different ages, abilities and personalities will not all want the same thing. Some people will happily throw themselves at a reaction wall; others would rather watch, chat and have a go at something quieter. Offering a range means nobody stands at the edge feeling left out.

      Be clear on what you want the event to do. ++[Exhibition stands](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/exhibition-games/)++ need fast footfall and easy conversation starters. ++[Conference programmes](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/conference-game-hire/)++ need something that resets attention between sessions. Team building days need activities that sustain engagement for hours and get people talking to colleagues they would not normally sit near. Those are three different briefs, and they point to three different game selections.

      Check the venue logistics before you finalise anything. Most of our games run from a standard mains socket and need roughly two to three metres of clear floor. The MK grid road layout and on site parking at the larger venues generally makes access manageable, though hotel function rooms can be tighter. Flag any constraint early and we can suggest an alternative that still does the job.

      If you want branding on the games, raise it when you ask for a quote. Custom wraps are designed and printed by our in-house team, and production needs lead time. Leaving it late limits what we can do.

      Finally, think about timing. Christmas is comfortably the busiest period in the Milton Keynes corporate calendar, and the better dates go early. If you are planning a festive event, our ++[Christmas entertainment](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/christmas-entertainment/)++ and ++[Christmas game hire](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/christmas-game-hire/)++ pages are a useful starting point, and it is sensible to start those conversations well before the autumn.
