Not sure which suits your event? It's worth thinking about the atmosphere you're after and the kind of guests you're hosting. Here's how the two compare.

Our ++[green screen photo booths](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/green-screen-photo-booths/)++ let guests choose their own backdrop, so the same booth can suit a relaxed birthday one week and a company celebration the next. Guests step in, pick a background, and walk away with a printed keepsake moments later. They work well for parties, family celebrations and corporate events where you want something interactive that keeps a queue moving.

### Magic mirror hire

Our [++magic mirror++](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/magic-mirror/) is a full-length, interactive experience where guests can sign and annotate their photos on screen before they print. It tends to suit bigger private parties and more formal occasions, where the mirror itself becomes a talking point as much as the photos it produces. If you'd like a hand deciding between the two, just ask; we're happy to talk it through based on your venue and guest numbers.

## Planning your photo booth hire: space, timing and venue access

A little planning ahead makes the day itself run more smoothly, and we're happy to guide you through it. A few things worth considering:

- **Space.** You'll want a clear area with easy access to a power socket. Set aside a comfortable corner and your guests will have room to gather without getting in the way of the rest of your event. 
- **Setup and collection.** Our team arrives ahead of your start time to set up, and we return to collect afterwards. This is handled separately, so it doesn't eat into your hire time.
- **Venue access.** It's worth checking load-in access, any lift restrictions and power availability with your venue in advance. If you let us know of anything tricky, we'll plan around it so there are no surprises on the day.

Every booth we supply is PAT-tested, and we carry full ++[public liability insurance](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/safety-and-insurance/)++, which many venues will ask to see.
