## **About the MTC as an Events Venue**

MTC Events sits within the Manufacturing Technology Centre campus at Ansty Business Park, on the northern edge of Coventry, just off Junction 2 of the M6. It's a modern, purpose-designed venue rather than a converted space, and the setting matters: this is a working research and technology campus at the heart of British manufacturing, which gives events held here a credibility that's hard to manufacture elsewhere.

For event planners, the useful figures are the spaces. The venue caters for anything from a two-person meeting up to around 350 attendees, across a 247-seat tiered auditorium, a bright open-plan exhibition space, a lakeside restaurant, a mezzanine, and a run of meeting rooms and business lounges. There's free on-site parking with roughly 300 spaces, Birmingham Airport is under 20 minutes away, and the venue has a strong sustainability commitment. Venue bookings are made directly through the MTC Events team.

## **Event Spaces at the MTC and the Games That Suit Them**

Different spaces work well for different kinds of entertainment. Once you know which room your event is in, we can recommend games that fit the footprint, power setup, and style of the occasion.

### **The Exhibition Space**

This is the natural home for interactive games at the MTC. The exhibition space is bright, open, and designed with flexibility in mind, and its bi-fold doors open directly onto the car park, which makes moving large items in and out considerably easier than at most venues. For us, that means straightforward load-in for bigger equipment. 

Given the audience the MTC attracts, racing car simulators tend to be the standout choice here, drawing a crowd as people compete for the fastest lap. ++[Batak Pro](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/coventry/batak-pro-hire/)++ and ++[Batak Lite](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/coventry/batak-lite/)++ reaction games, ++[Mega Buzz Wire](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/giant-buzz-wire)++, and ++[Grab A Grand](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/grab-a-grand/)++ cash cubes all work well too, particularly if you're one exhibitor among several and need something that stops passers-by. 

### **The Restaurant and Mezzanine**

The lakeside restaurant is the heart of the venue, with natural light, terrace access, and enough room for gala dinners and conference lunches. It can also be reconfigured for product launches and breakout sessions. The mezzanine offers a similar flexibility on a smaller scale, and works well for networking, informal breakouts, and refreshment breaks. Both suit post-dinner and between-session entertainment. Think ++[photo booths](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/photo-booths-and-magic-mirrors/)++, ++[prize wheels](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/prize-wheel/)++, shuffleboard, and smaller ++[retro arcade machines](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/arcade-games/)++ that add energy without dominating the room or interrupting conversation.

### **The Auditorium and Meeting Rooms**

The 247-seat auditorium is a tiered, fixed-seat theatre, so it isn't a space for games in itself. What it does mean is that conference days at the MTC tend to have clear break points, and that's where entertainment earns its place, positioned in the exhibition space or mezzanine just outside so people come across it naturally between sessions.

The meeting rooms and business lounges range from small syndicate rooms up to larger flexible spaces, with rooms 1 and 2 able to open into a single pillar-free room. Space here is more contained, so compact games work best. ++[Pub games](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/pub-games/)++, smaller arcades, and prize wheels all fit neatly. Several rooms overlook the lake, which is worth keeping in mind when thinking about layout and guest flow.

### **Outdoor Spaces**

The MTC has outdoor exhibition space available, and the restaurant terrace offers another option in good weather. If any part of your event moves outdoors, it's worth thinking about weather cover early. Most of our games are built for indoor use, though we do have options that travel outdoors safely. ++[Fun fair stalls](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/fun-fair-stalls/)++ like ++[hook-a-duck](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/hook-a-duck/)++ or a ++[coconut shy](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/coconut-shy-stall-hire/)++ can work well outdoors if there's shelter available. ## **Planning Entertainment for an MTC Event**

A few practical things are worth thinking about early. Once the MTC has confirmed your room, it's helpful to share the floor plan with us. That way we can advise on which games fit comfortably, where power points sit relative to your layout, and how to keep walkways clear for guest flow.

Because the MTC draws a lot of manufacturing and engineering audiences, it's worth thinking about what suits the room. Competitive, skill-based games tend to land particularly well with technical delegates, and they give people a reason to linger and talk, which is usually the point.

If you're exhibiting, branding is worth raising early too. Our in-house team can apply your logo and colours to your chosen game, which makes a noticeable difference on a busy floor, but it needs a bit of lead time.

In terms of lead time generally, two to four weeks' notice is usually comfortable for most games. If your event falls in November or December, we'd recommend booking earlier, as those dates tend to fill up first. That said, because we're so close by, we can often help at shorter notice than we could elsewhere. It's always worth asking.
