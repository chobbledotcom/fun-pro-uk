## About Royal Armouries and New Dock Hall as an Events Venue

The venue sits on Armouries Drive at Leeds Dock, on the waterfront development just south of the city centre. It's the largest dedicated ++[conference](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/conference-idea/)++, ++[exhibition](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/exhibition-games/)++ and events space in Leeds, hosting more than 380 national and international events a year and taking up to 2,500 attendees across the site.

The practical details tend to matter more than the headline figure. There's a multi-storey car park on site for 1,650 vehicles, around 850 hotel bedrooms within a five-minute walk, and 16 bookable spaces ranging from a 20-person boardroom to a 1,500-capacity hall. Leeds railway station is a short hop away, and Leeds Bradford Airport is around 30 minutes by road.

Bookings go through the venue's events team on 0113 220 1990.

### Royal Armouries Hall and the Bury Theatre

Royal Armouries Hall sits just inside the museum's main entrance on the ground floor, covering 886 square metres and taking 1,000 for a reception, 650 for dinner or 600 theatre-style. It is divided into three sound-proofed sections and links directly to the Bury Theatre on one side. 

The Bury itself is a fixed, tiered lecture theatre for up to 250, so it's a presentation space rather than a games space. If your plenary is in the Bury, the games belong next door in Armouries Hall, where attendees pass through between sessions. Reaction games like ++[Batak Pro](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/batak-pro/)++ and ++[Strike a Light](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/danger-zone-strike-a-light-game-hire/)++ do well in that position, drawing people in during breaks.

### The Museum Galleries

This is where the venue differs from anywhere else in Leeds. The Tournament Gallery (200 for a reception), the War Gallery (400) and the Asian and African Gallery (300) are all live museum galleries, hired for receptions and themed evenings among the collections. 

Games can absolutely go in, but the footprint, positioning and any cabling need agreeing with the venue in advance, because you're working around display cases and visitor routes. Compact and self-contained is the way to go here. ++[Shuffleboard](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/shuffleboard-hire/)++, a ++[Prize Wheel](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/prize-wheel/)++ or a ++[photo booth](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/photo-booths-and-magic-mirrors/)++ will sit comfortably; a racing simulator probably won't. We'll tell you honestly which is which.

### The Pearl Suite and Smaller Rooms

The Pearl Suite on the first floor takes 200 for a reception or 168 for dinner, with floor-to-ceiling windows over Leeds Dock. Below that sit the News Room (300), the Mulberry Suite (100), and the Aire, Barton and Hotot Suites, which handle 25 to 60 people for meetings and breakouts. 

The suites are working meeting rooms rather than entertainment spaces, so if your day is spread across several of them, we'd suggest siting one or two games in the Pearl Suite or an adjacent circulation area where everyone converges. Note that the Pearl Suite was previously called the Wellington Suite, and some third-party listings still use the old name.

## Planning Entertainment for a Royal Armouries Event

Your first question should be which building you're in, because the answer changes everything. New Dock Hall and Royal Armouries Hall are both ground floor with loading bays, so setup is quick and the equipment list is wide open. The museum galleries and upper-floor suites are a different proposition, with lifts, visitor routes and, in the galleries, exhibits to work around.

Share your floor plan early and we'll come back with a realistic list rather than a hopeful one. It's also worth flagging if you're using a gallery, since the venue will want to agree positioning in advance and we'd rather build that into the plan than discover it on the day.

Lead times run about four to six weeks for busy periods, including the Christmas season, when New Dock Hall and Armouries Hall both run party nights. Simpler bookings can often be arranged in around two weeks. ++[Branded games](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/branded-exhibition-games/)++ need a bit longer, since we design and print the wraps in-house.

With events here often running to several hundred attendees across a wide age and ability range, a spread of games works better than one big centrepiece. Something like ++[Roll & Bowl](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/categories/roll-and-bowl/)++ alongside a reaction game covers most of the room.
