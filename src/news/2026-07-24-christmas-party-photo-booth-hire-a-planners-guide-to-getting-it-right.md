A Christmas party photo booth is one of the few pieces of entertainment that works for a whole room rather than the confident half of it. But it works best when it has been planned around your venue and your guests, not simply added to the booking. This guide covers guest numbers, where to put it, props and branding, when to book, and what to do with the photos afterwards.

## **Why a Photo Booth Works at a Work Christmas Party**

Office ++[Christmas parties](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/christmas-entertainment/)++ have a particular challenge. You've got teams who see each other daily, teams who have never met, and a handful of people who would rather be anywhere else. Left to their own devices, everyone talks to the colleagues they already know.

A photo booth gives people something to do. That sounds simple, but it's the reason it works. Nobody has to introduce themselves or make small talk; they just get in the booth together and something happens.

What's more, it tends to draw in the people who hold back from everything else. The quieter members of the team often watch from a distance at first. Then a queue forms, someone from their team waves them over, and they're in. It's one of the few activities where reluctance turns into participation without anyone being singled out.

There's also the takeaway. A dance floor serves the dancers. A booth serves everyone, and everyone leaves with a print. Those photos end up on desks, on fridge doors, and pinned to office noticeboards, which means your event carries on well past December.

## **How Many Guests Can One Booth Actually Handle?**

This is the question that gets skipped, and it's the one that decides whether the booth works or frustrates people.

Our ++[Christmas photo booth](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/christmas-photo-booths/)++ takes groups of one to eight, which makes it well suited to team photos rather than individual portraits. Sessions typically run 20 to 30 minutes per group, giving people time for several poses and a proper rummage through the prop box.

Think about what that means for your numbers. A hundred guests moving through in groups of six is roughly seventeen visits, and people will want more than one go. Unlimited visits are included with the hire, which is generous, but it also means the booth stays busy all evening.

As a rough guide:

- **Up to 100 guests:** one booth is usually plenty, particularly if the party runs several hours
- **100 to 150 guests:** one booth still works, but consider a second focal point so people have somewhere to go while they wait
- **150 or more:** pair the booth with another attraction. A ++[Magic Mirror](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/magic-mirror/)++ gives you a second photo experience with a different feel, while something like ++[Whack an Elf](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/whack-an-elf/)++ or a ++[Christmas Prize Crane](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/christmas-prize-crane/)++ draws off the queue entirely

## **Where to Put the Booth in Your Venue**

The booth measures 2.7m x 2.0m x 2.05m, and you need to plan for more than that. A queue needs somewhere to stand, and people need room to sort out props before they get in.

It requires a level indoor surface with one UK mains socket within reach, drawing 500W for the camera, lighting and printer.

One point to check before you book anything: the booth cannot be carried up or down flights of stairs. If your function room isn't on the ground floor, you need lift access. This is worth confirming with your venue at the enquiry stage rather than discovering it on the day, and it's a conversation we'd much rather have with you eight weeks out than eight hours out.

On placement, a few things are worth considering:

- Keep it out of the direct route between the bar and the dance floor, otherwise the queue becomes an obstacle
- Move it away from the top table if there are speeches or awards; a booth in full flow doesn't pair well with a CEO thanking the finance team
- Foyer and drinks reception areas work particularly well. There's a natural lull before dinner, and the booth fills it

It's also worth asking your venue about loading access and setup windows. We typically arrive one to two hours before your event starts, and knowing about goods lifts, corridors or time restrictions in advance makes that straightforward.

## **Props, Backdrops and Themes That Suit Your Company**

![](/images/photo-booth-hire-fun-pro-uk.png)

Every hire includes a free prop box with wigs, hats and glasses, and the booth uses green screen technology with over 1,000 ++[custom branded backdrop](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/branded-game-hire/)++ options. That's a lot of choice, which is a good problem to have and a slightly paralysing one.

The useful principle is to match the backdrop and props to the tone of your party rather than defaulting to the loudest festive option available. A black-tie awards evening and a relaxed office party call for very different photos.

Theme directions worth thinking about:

- **Winter wonderland** – snow, pine, soft lighting; works for almost any audience
- **Festive black-tie** – clean and elegant, suits awards evenings and client-facing events
- **Après-ski** – warmer and more informal, good for a younger or more social team
- **Christmas market** – bustling and characterful, pairs well with fun food carts
- **Branded backdrop** – your colours and logo, for events where clients or partners are in the room

A word of caution on props. Props that involve people wearing something silly land brilliantly with some teams and awkwardly with others. Think about who's actually in the room. If senior stakeholders or clients are attending, a lighter touch tends to serve you better, and the prop box works just as well as an option people can ignore.

## **Branding the Booth, and Why It's Worth Doing**

Prints can carry your logo, a festive message, or your event details, and the booth exterior can be personalised or branded to match. We handle branding in-house: we print it, apply it, and remove it after the event.

The practical point is the deadline. Artwork needs to reach us around six to eight weeks before your event so our print workshop has time to turn it around properly.

The strategic point is worth spelling out, because it's easy to miss. Every print that leaves the room is a small piece of brand collateral that someone has chosen to take home and put somewhere visible. That's a very different proposition from a photographer's gallery link that nobody opens.

Keep it light, though. A logo on a print should read as a memento of the night, not an advert. Discreet works better than dominant, and guests are far more likely to keep it. You can find more on how we approach this on our ++[brand activation](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/events/brand-activation/)++ page.

## **What to Do With the Photos Afterwards**

You'll get a digital copy of every photo taken. Most planners file it and forget it, which is a shame, because it's genuinely useful.

Consider using them for:

- Internal newsletters and the intranet
- Your careers page, where real photos of real colleagues do more than stock imagery ever will
- Promoting next year's event, which is far easier when people can see what they missed
- Recruitment and onboarding packs

One thing to get right first, particularly if you're in HR. Guests posing in a booth at a Christmas party haven't necessarily agreed to appear on your company's careers page. It's worth signposting at the event how the photos might be used, and giving people a straightforward way to opt out. A brief note on the invite and a sign near the booth covers it, and it saves an awkward conversation in January.

December is the busiest period of our year, and December Fridays and Saturdays go first. Here's a timeline that works:

### **8 to 10 weeks before**

Confirm your venue, guest numbers and budget. Check availability for your date. This is the point where you still have real choice.

### **6 to 8 weeks before**

Finalise the booking and send over any branding artwork, so there's time for our print workshop.

### **4 weeks before**

Confirm logistics: ++[delivery](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/locations/)++ access, setup time, power supply, and lift access if you need it.

### **1 week before**

Agree arrival time, setup duration and collection arrangements.

## **When a Photo Booth Isn't the Right Call**

![](/images/photo-booth-hire.png)

Not every event suits a booth, and we'd rather tell you that than take a booking that disappoints you.

- **Upstairs function room with no lift.** The booth can't manage stairs. A Magic Mirror has a different footprint and may work where the booth can't, so it's worth a conversation.
- **A very short event.** A one-hour drinks reception doesn't give the booth a fair run, and you'll have paid for something half your guests never reached. Compact games like ++[Strike a Snowflake](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/strike-a-snow-flake/)++ or ++[Candy Cane Megawire](https://www.funprouk.co.uk/products/candy-cane-megawire/)++ deliver more in a short window.
- **A room where the only free floor space is a walkway.** A booth in a thoroughfare creates a bottleneck rather than a focal point.

In each case there's usually a better fit rather than no fit. Tell us the constraint and we'll work around it.
